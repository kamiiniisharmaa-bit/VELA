How VELA came to be

Founded in 2021 between Los Angeles and Kyoto, VELA started as a quiet experiment — blending Japanese material sensitivity with California ease. We noticed how many objects in our homes felt temporary, disposable. So we set out to craft pieces that would age beautifully, carrying stories over decades.

Our name "VELA" (meaning "sail" in several languages) reflects our journey: moving with intention, guided by craft, light, and texture. Every piece we make is designed to be held, used, and loved without hurry.

Our guiding principles

🌱

Slow craft

We work with small-batch artisans, favoring traditional techniques over mass production. Each piece carries subtle individuality.

🪶

Radical longevity

Designed to be repaired, restored, and passed on. We offer lifetime care for every VELA object.

🌍

Regenerative materials

From recycled aluminum to FSC-certified timber — we prioritize low-impact, ethically sourced resources.
